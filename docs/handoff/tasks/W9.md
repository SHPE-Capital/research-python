| Wave | Level | Depends on | Blocks | Agent |

## Goal

Take one signal from each team and decide holding period, stop, target and
sizing from evidence: holding curves, MFE/MAE, stop/target curves and
parameter surfaces with the trial count shown.

## Interface

Three sections on fixtures:

| Section | Fixture | Signal | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Momentum | `synthetic_bars(momentum=..., fat tail)` | SM1 | Which stop width? (stop curve) |
| Reversals | `synthetic_bars(reversal={"ar": AR(5)})` | SR1, SR2 | How long to hold? (holding curve) |
| Stat arb | `synthetic_bars(cointegrated_pairs=[OU pair])` | SS4 | Max hold in half-lives (SS3) |

The last exercise exports SR1 with T7 and reads its parity report.

## Behaviour

- Every curve and surface prints `n_trials` (T6).
- All runs use the T5 default cost; one exercise swaps a calibrated model.
