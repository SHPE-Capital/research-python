## Goal

Find pairs with PCA and OPTICS clustering instead of searching every pair,
compare that against distance, correlation and all-pairs selection, and trade
the survivors with the platform-parity pairs strategy out of sample.

## Interface

Data: `synthetic_bars(factors={"market": 1, "sectors": 3},
cointegrated_pairs=[{"beta": 1.2, "half_life_bars": 12}, {"beta": 0.8,
"half_life_bars": 30}], trap=True, null_symbols=...)`. Formation window,
then trading window. Report per method: `n_tested`, planted pairs found,
trap rejected, out-of-sample net P&L with a bootstrap band (E2).

## Behaviour

- Spreads on raw prices (the platform's price space). A log-price variant is
  an exercise and is labelled not exportable (P9).
- Selection uses the formation window only; trading uses SS4 with
  `hedge: rolling_ols`.
