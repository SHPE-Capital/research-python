## Goal

Forecast next-day realised variance with the HAR model (Corsi, 2009) and with
gradient boosting on the same inputs, and show that volatility is far more
predictable than returns.

## Interface

Data: `synthetic_bars(vol_clustering={"persistence": 0.95})` with 6 symbols
over about 2 years, resampled to daily (C1e). Label `forward_realized_var`
(C1c, next session). Features `har_rv` (`rv_d`, `rv_w`, `rv_m`). HAR is a
linear regression (`ridge_reg` with alpha 0, or OLS in `ex05_har_vs_gbm.py`);
GBM is `lightgbm_reg` (M1b). Split: `walk_forward` (C1f), expanding.

Metrics: out-of-sample R² of variance and QLIKE loss, each with a block
bootstrap band (E2).

## Behaviour

- Realised variance from minute returns within the regular session only.
- Expected outcome (hypothesis): both models have positive out-of-sample R²
  on the fixture; HAR is within GBM's band.
