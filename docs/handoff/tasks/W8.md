## Goal

Show that a signal with a real edge can still be mostly market beta and
sector bets, and that neutralisation, sizing schemes and turnover control
change the result more than the model does.

## Interface

Data: `synthetic_bars(daily={"years": 2, "n_symbols": 60},
factors={"market": 1, "sectors": 3}, xs_alpha={"ic": 0.05,
"contaminate": {"beta": 0.5, "sector": 0.5}})`. The contaminated score is
part alpha, part beta, part sector.

Configs vary the `portfolio` block (T9): `scheme` (`top_k`, `rank`,
`zscore`, `vol_scaled`), `neutralize` (none, `[beta]`, `[beta, sector]`),
`turnover.no_trade_band`, `turnover.partial_adjustment`. Risk limits use
snake_case `RiskLimits` names (T4), not the platform's camelCase.

## Behaviour

- Beta from F8 on past bars only; sector from the fixture's planted labels.
- Report per config: gross and net Sharpe, turnover, |β|, sector exposure,
  and the t-stat of the residual return after regressing on market and sectors.
