## What changes

- Two exports now exist. M3 exports a **model** (`model.onnx`,
  `manifest.json`, golden rows). [T7](T7.md) exports a **strategy config**
  (`deploy/platform_config.py`). A meta-labelled strategy ships both: the
  platform config's `metaModelPath` (P7, P8, P9) points at M3's output.
- `shpe export <run_dir>/model` stays as the stub specifies. It is added
  through its own `_add_<group>` function (see the `cli.py` hotspot in the
  [handoff README](../README.md)).
- Manifest feature names must be ones the platform produces; the parity rules
  are in [design/platform-parity.md](../../design/platform-parity.md).
