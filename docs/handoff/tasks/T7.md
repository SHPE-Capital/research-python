## Goal

Turn a `StrategySpec` into a platform strategy config and backtest config, run
both engines on the same bars, and report every trade that differs. A strategy
is not handed to the platform until its parity report passes.

## Interface

`export_platform_config(spec: StrategySpec) -> PlatformExport` with
`strategy` (type and params) and `backtest` (a `BacktestConfig` fragment:
`fillModel`, `slippageBps`, `commissionPerShare`, `riskConfig`,
`dataGranularity: "bar"`, `startDate`, `endDate`, starting cash from
`initial_equity`).

`compare_ledgers(research: TradeLedger, platform_trades: list[RoundTrip]) -> ParityReport`.
`RoundTrip` fields as the platform returns them: `symbol`, `direction`, `qty`,
`entryTs`, `exitTs`, `entryPrice`, `exitPrice`, `pnl`, `commission`, `holdingMs`.

`ParityReport`: `matched`, `research_only`, `platform_only`, `mismatches`
(field, research value, platform value, first divergence bar), `passed`.

## Behaviour

Export:

- Supported types: `minute_reversal` (SR1) and `pairs_trading` (SS4).
  Everything else is refused by name: momentum until P8, SR2/SR3 until P7,
  Kalman or TLS hedge ratios until P9, non-exportable sizing rules (T2),
  `max_concurrent_same_side` (T4), any filter (T3) without a platform field.
- Durations convert to Ms (`rollingWindowMs`, `cooldownMs`, `maxHoldingMs`).
- `session_end` must be False in the spec: neither `minute_reversal` nor
  `pairs_trading` flattens at the close on the platform. Export refuses
  `session_end=True`.
- `priceSource` is written for the record but the platform ignores it since v5.
- `initial_equity` has no platform risk field; it maps to the backtest's
  starting cash and the mapping is printed in the report.

Comparison:

- Trades match on `(symbol, direction, entry time)`.
- Entry and exit timestamps, `exit_reason` and `qty` must match exactly;
  prices within 1e-6 relative; commission within 1e-6 absolute.
- `passed` only with zero research-only, zero platform-only and zero mismatches.
- Commission check: confirm whether `commissionPerShare` reaches platform P&L.
  `fillModel.ts` describes it as "accounting only". If `pnl` excludes it, the
  comparison uses `pnl` + `commission` and the report says so; the finding is
  written into [design/platform-parity.md](../../design/platform-parity.md)
  through its owner.

Golden sessions (`tests/integration/golden/<family>/<date>/`): at least five
per family: an ordinary session, an open gap, an early close (e.g. 2026-11-27),
missing or zero-volume minutes, and a session where a risk limit binds. Pairs
adds a session where cointegration flips. Each folder holds the input bars,
the spec, the platform ledger (exported JSON) and the expected research ledger.

## Out of scope

Platform code changes (P6–P9), running the platform in CI (goldens hold its
exported output), live trading.

## Done when

Unit tests pass, SR1 and SS4 pass all their golden sessions, and the commission
finding is recorded.
