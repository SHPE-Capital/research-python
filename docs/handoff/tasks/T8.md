| Wave | Level | Depends on | Blocks | Agent |

## Goal

Give every strategy a one-page card that states what it trades, why, how it
exits and what evidence supports it, and give teams a `shpe new strategy`
template that produces a working, testable strategy folder.

## Owns (exclusive)

- `src/shpe_research/scaffold/templates/strategy/` (all files)
- `src/shpe_research/strategies/card.py` (`render_card(spec, run_dir) -> str`)
- `tests/unit/scaffold/test_strategy_template.py`

## Reads

- [C1j.md](C1j.md) (`shpe new` framework; C1j owns it and the feature, model and study templates)
- [design/architecture.md](../../design/architecture.md), [design/trade-layer.md](../../design/trade-layer.md)

- [research/spec-card-template.md](../../research/spec-card-template.md) (the card format; `render_card` follows its sections and does not redefine them)

## Interface

`shpe new strategy <team> <name>` creates:

| Path | Content |
|---|---|
| `src/shpe_research/strategies/<team>/<name>.py` | A `CandidateGenerator` stub registered under `<name>` |
| `configs/strategies/<team>/<name>.yaml` | A `StrategySpec` with `exit_policy`, `sizing`, `filters`, `risk`, `costs` |
| `tests/unit/strategies/<team>/test_<name>.py` | Calls `check_strategy` (C1k) on a fixture |
| `studies/<team>/<id>-<name>/README.md` | Card stub plus the research-protocol checklist |

The card sections: thesis, universe, candidate rule, filters, exit policy,
sizing, risk, costs (with `source`), evidence (run IDs, deflated Sharpe,
trial count, held-out result), parity status (T7 report or "not exportable:
<reason>"), known failure modes.

## Behaviour

- The generated strategy passes `check_strategy` and its test before any edit.
- `render_card` fills evidence from the run directory and refuses to mark
  "held-out" unless the manifest records a held-out run.
- The template's config uses the platform default fill and cost model.

## Acceptance tests

| Path | Test | Asserts |
|---|---|---|
| `tests/unit/scaffold/test_strategy_template.py` | `test_new_strategy_runs` | Generated strategy's test passes on the fixture |
