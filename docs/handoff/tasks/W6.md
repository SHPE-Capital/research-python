## Goal

Participants search a strategy grid on pure noise, find a "great" backtest,
and then watch the deflated Sharpe ratio (Bailey and López de Prado, 2014)
and the probability of backtest overfitting (Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado
and Zhu, 2017) reject it.

## Interface

Data: `synthetic_bars(signal_strength=0, symbols=["N01", ..., "N10"])` (no
signal). Strategy: `range_breakout` events (C1b) through T1 with a grid over
range length, stop, target and hold. The notebook reports the best train
Sharpe, `n_trials`, the deflated Sharpe of the best point and PBO from CSCV.

## Behaviour

- The grid runs through M2 so `n_trials` is recorded automatically.
- Costs are the T5 default; an exercise reruns with zero cost to show how much
  of the "edge" was cost-free fantasy.
